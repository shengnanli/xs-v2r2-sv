# OpenNCB — CHI<->AXI Non-Coherent Bridge parent assembly

`OpenNCB` is the parent of `NCB200`. It is a **pure combinational assembly shell**
with no state of its own.

## Golden structure (`OpenNCB.sv`, 2669 lines)

- **1 child instance**: `NCB200 uNCB200` (the whole bridge datapath lives here).
- **0 registers, 0 `always` blocks, 0 `_GEN_`/`_T_`** — no sequential logic, no
  datapath in the top module.
- **Inbound CHI flit vectors** (`io_chi_tx_req_flit[161:0]`,
  `io_chi_tx_dat_flit[421:0]`) are bit-sliced into NCB200's individual field ports
  (e.g. `io_chi_rxreq_flit_Opcode = io_chi_tx_req_flit[68:62]`).
- **Outbound CHI flit vectors** (`io_chi_rx_rsp_flit[72:0]`,
  `io_chi_rx_dat_flit[421:0]`) are formed by **2 concat assigns** repacking
  NCB200's `txrsp`/`txdat` field outputs (captured on 25 wires) with the
  appropriate zero-padding.
- **AXI ports** (`auto_axi4_out_*`) pass straight through to NCB200's `io_axi_*`.
- All **1143 `debug_*` outputs** of NCB200 are left `/* unused */` (as in golden).

The connectivity IS the spec — golden is already firtool-preamble-only Verilog
with zero `_GEN_`. The readable core `xs_OpenNCB_core`
(`rtl/uncore/OpenNCB.sv`, generated by `scripts/gen_openncb.py`) is a faithful,
grouped, commented, bug-for-bug structural reconstruction of golden connectivity.
